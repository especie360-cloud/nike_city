Nike City is a living map where every module becomes a small experience: discover a zone, interact with it, collect a story and keep moving.

The flow

From map to movement in four steps.

01

Land in the city

Start from the central map and rotate, zoom or move around the isometric world.

02

Pick a district

Tap a pin to open zones like Soccer, Basketball, Running, Casual, Skate Park or Metro.

03

Unlock the layer

Each module can reveal products, stories, challenges, rewards or guided routes.

04

Keep moving

Follow paths between districts and build your own way through Nike City.
